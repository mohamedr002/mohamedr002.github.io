I am a **Senior Researcher** at the [Technology Innovation Institute (TII)](https://www.tii.ae/), UAE, and an **Adjunct Researcher** at [A\*STAR](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/), Singapore. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from [Nanyang Technological University (NTU)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), Singapore in 2022.

{% if site.industrial_intelligence_lab_enabled %}
I lead the **[Industrial Intelligence Lab](/industrial-intelligence-lab/)** — building the full stack of industrial AI from sensor signals to autonomous decisions.
{% endif %}

**Research focus:**

- **Foundation models** for industrial time series and prognostics
- **Physics-informed deep learning** for trustworthy predictions on physical assets
- **Generative AI and agentic systems** for industrial operations
- **Intelligent health monitoring** of jet engines, drones, ships, and manufacturing assets
- **Label-efficient and privacy-preserving learning** under distribution shift
